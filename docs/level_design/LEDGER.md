## Decisions

- 2026-11-06 — the level is added as a **third map**; the two existing maps, the tutorial and the flow stay intact.
- 2026-11-06 — the level's rewards are **positional** (height, cover, rotation speed): the game has no item pickups
  and inventing one is out of scope for a level-design deliverable.
- 2026-11-06 — scenario = abandoned substation yard; topology = three nested loops + two chokepoints; the elevated
  loop is broken by a corner mast that only Scissors' wall climb closes.
- 2026-11-06 — `layout.json` is the single source of truth: the blueprint, the metrics and (next) the ProBuilder
  builder all read it, so the drawing cannot drift from the level.
